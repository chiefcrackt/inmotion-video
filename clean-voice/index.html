Clean Voice

Remove background noise and bring your voice forward in a video or audio recording, using your computer’s NVIDIA graphics card.

Computer only — local launcher required

This tool does not work on phones or tablets. Open it on the Windows computer running the Clean Voice launcher.

Computer only

Open your local studio

  1. On your Windows computer, open the repo’s local-voice folder and double-click Start Clean Voice.cmd. The first start installs the engines and can take several minutes.
  2. It runs in the background, with no window to keep open. Open the studio below; its Stop button at the bottom ends it.
  3. Choose a recording, pick a mode, compare before and after, and download the cleaned video or WAV.

Open Clean Voice ↗

If the page cannot connect, start the launcher first. The address opens a service on the device you are using. Your phone cannot use the engine on your PC.

Modes

Remove noise
DeepFilterNet takes out fans, hum, wind, traffic and room noise while keeping the voice natural. Light, Medium and Strong set how far the noise is pushed down.
Isolate voice
Demucs pulls the voice away from music or a busy background, then removes what noise is left.
Finish
A rumble filter and loudness evening (−16 LUFS, a good level for social video) so every clip comes out at the same volume.

Videos keep their original picture: only the sound track is replaced. Recordings stay on your computer. First use downloads the models; later runs work offline.
