# Recorded samples

## steel-guitar
Ten steel-string acoustic guitar notes (E2 to E5 roots), used by Beat Bed's
**Acoustic Adventure** style.

- Source: **GeneralUser GS v2.0.3** by S. Christian Collins, bank 0 program 25
  ("Steel Guitar"). https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser
- License: GeneralUser GS may be used without restriction for music creation,
  private or commercial, and in software projects. Per the author's request,
  this repo hosts its own copy rather than linking to his download files.
- Each note was pre-rendered as a 3.5 s one-shot at velocity 105, following the
  preset's loop, volume envelope and velocity filter sweep, then encoded to
  80 kbps mono MP3. Rebuild with `scripts/samples/render_guitar.py`.
