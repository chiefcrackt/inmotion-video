T.im RUNNING ESSENTIALS

15 assets: six training labels, four transitions, five distance markers.
1920 x 1080 landscape / 1080 x 1920 portrait. 30 fps.

GreenScreen.mp4: H.264 + AAC sound. In CapCut use Chroma Key on green.
Alpha.webm: VP9 alpha + Opus sound. Use an editor supporting WebM transparency; iPhone Photos does not.
Preview.png: navy-backed reference still, not a transparent overlay.
WAV: matching stereo 48 kHz PCM sound, one per asset. Video already includes sound; mute it before adding WAV separately.

Transitions: place over your cut, align the cut to 0.70 seconds from the clip start. All four cover the complete frame at that point. Keep original duration and position.
Labels and distance cards enter, hold, then leave; first and final frames are clear.

Individual clips and this ZIP are ready-made files, no rendering needed.
Preview montage shows all 15 landscape clips on navy.
