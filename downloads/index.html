Animation Downloads

The finished animations, ready for CapCut. Every video has its sound built in, and the sound is also here on its own as a WAV.

On iPhone: use the MP4 buttons and pick Save Video in the share sheet to put the clip in Photos. Overlays come as green screen MP4: in CapCut, add it as an overlay and use Chroma Key on the green. The transparent WebM files are for editors on a computer; iPhone Photos can’t open them. The big ProRes desktop files aren’t here.
