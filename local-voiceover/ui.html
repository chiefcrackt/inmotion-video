T.IM / LOCAL STUDIO

Voiceover

Turn a script into a voiceover with Kokoro, an open text-to-speech model. You get a WAV for CapCut and word timing for Caption Maker. Everything stays on this computer.

Script

Start a line with a name and a colon, like Coach:, to give that speaker their own voice. A line of [pause 1.5] adds 1.5 seconds of silence.

Voiceover

Paste a script to begin.

    Voice

    T.im narrator (60% Puck + 40% Michael) is the default. Grades are from Kokoro's own voice list; Heart and Bella are the most natural stock voices.

    Speakers

    Lines without a name use the voice above.

    Blend voices

    Mix two or three voices into a new one. It stays the same on every line.

      Pronunciation

      Words the voice says wrong: write how it should sound, like Tim, or exact phonemes between slashes, like /kˈOkəɹO/. Captions still show the word as written.

      Results are stored in local-voiceover/data on this computer, with a cache of every line spoken so unchanged lines are reused. Remove old folders there when you no longer need them.

      It runs in the background with no window. Stop it when you're done; Start Voiceover.cmd starts it again.
