Beat Bed

A music bed for your videos, made from the same synth kit as the data videos. Pick a style (each says what it’s good for), a mood and a groove, shape the sound, lay out the song in sections, preview it, then save a WAV and drop it under your clip in CapCut. Everything lands on whole bars, so it ends on the beat.

Mixer and sound changes apply live while it plays. Bar lines on the preview are where cuts land on the beat. On iPhone, Save WAV opens the share sheet: pick Save to Files, then add it in CapCut from your files.
